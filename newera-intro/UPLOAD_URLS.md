## Project Summary

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (landscape), 1080x1920 (vertical)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps
- **Duration**: ~2:20 (synced to Life Force beat grid)
- **Audio**: Life Force track embedded
- **Codec**: H.264 (MP4), VP9 (WebM)

### Full Vibe Aesthetic Implementation
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif with balanced kerning
- **Color Palette**: High-contrast black/white with cyan accent
- **Motion**: Fluid ease-in/ease-out animations
- **Geometric Shapes**: Circles, squares, triangles, lines
- **Transitions**: Beat-synced cuts and smooth morphs

### Beat-Synced Composition
- All cuts aligned to Life Force BPM (128 BPM)
- Visual events on downbeats (1, 2, 3, 4)
- Subdivisions on 8th/16th notes for fast cuts
- Frequency-reactive geometric scaling

### Analysis Documents
- `MANUS_2.0_ANALYSIS.md` - Shot-by-shot analysis of Manus 2.0 intro
- `LIFE_FORCE_BEAT_ANALYSIS.md` - Spectrum/frequency/beat analysis of Life Force track
- `FULL_VIBE_STYLE_GUIDE.md` - Design system documentation
- `MAKING_OF.md` - Technical implementation details
